## How this skill applies the practice

- Frames every resolution as a decision by named people with authority, prepared with facts and options, so the result survives the meeting and the people who were not in it.
- Matches the technique to the conflict type found during analysis (agreement, compromise, voting, variants, overruling) and keeps analysis aids (consider-all-facts, plus-minus-interesting, decision matrix) separate from the decision itself.
- Enforces the order that makes a decision matrix honest: criteria and weights are agreed and recorded before any option is scored.
- Documents the resolution with its assumptions, constraints, alternatives, reasons, decision-makers and dissent, in a format that architecture decision tooling already reads.
- Carries the decision into the requirements themselves (status, links, rewritten text, rejected rows) only after confirmation, so the register and the record never disagree.

## What this skill deliberately leaves out

- Finding and classifying conflicts: `conflict-detector`.
- Handling relationship and structural conflicts, which go to escalation, not to a resolution session.
- Changing baselined requirements under change control: `change-impact`.
