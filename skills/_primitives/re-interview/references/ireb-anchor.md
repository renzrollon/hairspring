## How this skill applies the practice

- Treats questioning as a cost: each question must be able to change the outcome.
- Uses W-questions and a recommended answer so the person can respond quickly and precisely.
- Confirms understanding by paraphrase in the glossary's vocabulary, which is how shared understanding is checked rather than assumed.
- Records every answer with its source so backward traceability starts at the moment of elicitation.

## What this skill deliberately leaves out

- Planning and running a full stakeholder interview: `interview-guide`.
- Choosing other elicitation techniques: `elicitation-workshop`, `observation-protocol`, `questionnaire-builder`.
