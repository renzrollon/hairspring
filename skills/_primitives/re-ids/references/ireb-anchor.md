## How this skill applies the practice

- Gives every requirement a unique, stable identifier at birth, which is the precondition for every trace link.
- Enforces a minimum attribute set so that source, priority and rationale are never reconstructed after the fact.
- Encodes the requirement life cycle as explicit, checkable transitions.

## What this skill deliberately leaves out

- Choosing extra attributes and lifecycle states for a team: `attribute-schema`.
- Building trace links: `traceability-matrix`.
