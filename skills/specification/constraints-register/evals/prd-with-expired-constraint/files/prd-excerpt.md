# PRD excerpt: Harbourline Rewards, section 6 "Boundaries"

- The new service must comply with our data retention policy DRP-2024 (S-05): Finance (Grace Lim, SH-03) keeps transaction data for 7 years for audit.
- Personal data must be deleted when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for (Farid Osman, data protection officer, SH-04, under the PDPA).
- We must go live before the legacy loyalty module's contract ends on 2027-03-31 (Grace Lim, SH-03).
- The service must be fast.
- The team prefers PostgreSQL for the transaction store.
