# Kick-off interview I-01, Mei Tan (SH-01, Head of Loyalty), 2026-10-27

Redemption:
- Members pick how many points to redeem in the app and get a voucher QR they show at the till.
- The cashier scans it, the POS checks it with us, and the discount goes on the bill.
- Fraud wants to see every redemption before a voucher is issued.
- A voucher can be used once. Unused vouchers expire; I don't know after how long yet.
- Programme terms (S-01): 100 points = SGD 1 voucher value, minimum redemption 500 points, points are redeemable once posted (24 h after purchase).
- Everything here is a must for launch.
