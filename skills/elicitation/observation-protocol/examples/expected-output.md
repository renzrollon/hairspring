## Focus questions

1. What happens at the till when a member's QR code cannot be scanned?
2. How are vouchers applied, and what happens when the till rejects one?
3. What do cashiers do about loyalty when the network drops?

## Debrief script

Same day, 20 min, with both cashiers together.

1. "I saw you ask members to turn up the brightness when the scan failed. Can you tell me about that?"
2. "What would happen if you didn't write the receipt numbers down?"
3. "How often does each of these happen in a normal shift or week?"
4. "If this worked the way you want, what would be different?"
5. "Was today a normal day? What usually happens that didn't today?"
6. "Did you do anything differently because I was watching?"
