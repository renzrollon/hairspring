## How this skill applies the practice

- Uses observation where questioning fails: tacit knowledge and routine workarounds that stakeholders no longer mention because they stopped noticing them.
- Chooses between watching (field observation) and doing the work under guidance (apprenticing) by the nature of the work and the risk of a novice doing it.
- Separates what was observed from what was reported, and checks both in a same-day debrief, which is how the observer's interpretation is confirmed by the people observed.
- Treats each workaround as evidence of an unmet need, typically a dissatisfier in Kano terms, and hands it to the process owner to confirm rather than turning it into a requirement directly.
- Keeps consent and the protection of customers' and staff's data as preconditions of the session, not as an afterthought.

## What this skill deliberately leaves out

- Drawing the observed process as a model: `process-flow`.
- Checking how widespread a finding is: `questionnaire-builder`.
- Final Kano classification and prioritization: `requirement-classifier`, `prioritization`.
