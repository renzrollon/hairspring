# Harbourline Rewards programme terms and conditions, draft v3 (S-01), excerpt

## 4. Earning points

4.1 Members earn 1 point for every SGD 1 spent in a single transaction. Points are rounded down per transaction.

4.2 Harbourline may run bonus campaigns that multiply the points earned during the campaign period.

4.3 Points become available 24 hours after the purchase. Until then they are pending and are cancelled if the purchase is returned.

## 6. Returns

6.2 If points from a returned purchase have already been redeemed, the member's balance may become negative. (Draft: to be confirmed by Finance.)
