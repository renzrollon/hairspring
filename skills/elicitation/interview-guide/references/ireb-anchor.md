## How this skill applies the practice

- Plans the interview around a few explicit goals, so the time goes to the information the next decision needs.
- Moves from open to closed questions, so stakeholders first show what matters to them and only then confirm specifics.
- Uses the quality model as a checklist for the role, which brings out the quality requirements and constraints that stakeholders assume and rarely mention.
- Confirms understanding by reading the main points back within the interview, and records which points were confirmed.
- Keeps the stakeholder's words, the interview ID and the capture row with every candidate, so backward traceability starts in the interview.

## What this skill deliberately leaves out

- Short question batches before implementing a task: `clarifying-questions`.
- Classifying and phrasing what was heard: `requirement-classifier`, `requirement-phrasing`.
- A full quality walkthrough with measurable targets: `quality-requirements`.
