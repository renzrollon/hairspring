## Facilitation notes

### Opening

- Say: "Today we collect ideas that would make redeeming points a pleasure. We judge nothing until the voting. One idea per note."
- Prepare: board frames for paradox, six 6-3-5 sheets, cluster area, the two Kano questions on a slide.
- If stuck or dominated: ask the members first in every round-robin.

### Warm-up: brainstorming paradox

- Say: "How could we make redeeming points as frustrating as possible?"
- After 10 minutes, invert each note together: "What is the need behind avoiding this?"
- If stuck or dominated: give one example ("make the cashier type a long code").

### Ideas: 6-3-5

- Say: "Write three ideas in your row in five minutes, then the sheets rotate. Read what is above you and build on it."
- Prepare: sheets pre-numbered 1 to 6 with three columns and six rows; timer visible to all.
- If stuck or dominated: allow a variation of an idea above; never skip a round.

### Cluster, vote, Kano

- Say: "Place your five dots on ideas, not on clusters. Votes stay hidden until everyone is done."
- For each idea with a dot: "How would members feel if we had it? And if we did not?"
- If stuck or dominated: tag `TBD` and move on; `requirement-classifier` refines it.

### Close

- Say: "Parked items get an owner now, or they are lost."
