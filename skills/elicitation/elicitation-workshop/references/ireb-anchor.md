## How this skill applies the practice

- Uses a workshop when the requirements do not yet exist in anyone's head and have to be created together, which is where creativity techniques earn their time.
- Chooses the technique from the goal and the group (equal voice, remote, seniority), instead of defaulting to open brainstorming.
- Separates diverging from converging, so ideas are generated before they are judged.
- Tags ideas with a first Kano category in the room, which helps find delighters and dissatisfiers early, and leaves the final classification to `requirement-classifier`.
- Lands every idea with its participants' words and a location in the capture table, so the trace from candidate back to the workshop survives.

## What this skill deliberately leaves out

- One-to-one elicitation: `interview-guide`.
- Deciding between solution options with weighted criteria: `conflict-to-decision`.
- Ranking the resulting candidates: `prioritization`.
