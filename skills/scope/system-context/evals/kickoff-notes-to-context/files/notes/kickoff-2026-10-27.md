# Kick-off notes, 2026-10-27 (Mei Tan, Head of Loyalty, SH-01)

- The loyalty-points service handles earning, balance and redemption, plus an admin console for support agents.
- The POS (TillPoint) sends us purchase events and asks us to validate vouchers.
- The customer mobile app shows balance and history from our API and lets members redeem points.
- The CRM (Relay) receives member tier and a points summary every night.
- The fraud engine gets our earn and redeem events and sends hold or release back.
- Notification provider: points earned and points expiring messages, SMS and push.
- Data warehouse: daily extract for Finance (points liability).
