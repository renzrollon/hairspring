## How this skill applies the practice

- Treats stakeholders as the most important kind of requirements source and makes the inventory explicit before elicitation, so missing voices are a visible gap rather than a late surprise.
- Separates the stakeholder (a role with goals) from its representative (the person who speaks for it now), and treats a role with nobody named as a gap.
- Rates influence and interest with yes-or-no rules so two people mapping the same project reach the same quadrant, and derives the engagement approach from it.
- Checks the inventory against a role checklist with triggers, which is how the roles that are rarely invited (fraud, legal, data protection, owners of neighbouring systems, people who lose something) surface.
- Records availability, because a stakeholder nobody can reach changes which elicitation technique is possible.

## What this skill deliberately leaves out

- Planning elicitation per source: `source-inventory`.
- Running interviews and workshops: `interview-guide`, `elicitation-workshop`.
- Resolving disagreements between stakeholders: `conflict-detector`, `conflict-to-decision`.
